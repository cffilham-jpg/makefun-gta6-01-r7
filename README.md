A sunlit coastal crime story set in Palma, a walkable open city of high-rises, quiet neighborhoods, beaches, and an old marina. Drive, steal a ride, take a fare, race the boardwalk, collect hidden packages, and make your way through twelve story chapters while the city answers your choices.

## Controls

- **WASD** or arrow keys: walk / drive
- **E**: enter a nearby car, leave your car, or interact with a shop
- **Mouse** or **F**: aim and fire on foot; **1–3**: change weapon
- **Space**: handbrake
- **M**: chapter select / replay
- **J**: take a taxi fare; **V**: start a vigilante shift
- **B**: shop; **R**: repair
- **Esc**: pause, save, or change settings

## Run locally

With Node.js 22 or later, run `node serve.ts` from the repository root and open http://localhost:8080. The game uses local files only.
